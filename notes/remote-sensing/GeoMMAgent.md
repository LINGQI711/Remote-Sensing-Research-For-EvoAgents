**Topic:** Expert multimodal geoscience reasoning

## 1. What problem does it address?

General multimodal models lack the specialized perception and scientific reasoning required across geoscience disciplines and remote sensing sensors.

## 2. How does it solve the problem?

GeoMMBench evaluates this broader expertise. GeoMMAgent combines retrieval, perception, and reasoning specialists with external remote sensing models, using a zero-shot agent framework rather than fine-tuning a new foundation model.

## 3. What experiments were conducted?

- Benchmarks 36 models and human participants across geoscience and remote sensing tasks.
- Compares the multi-agent system with general multimodal baselines and analyzes its specialist components.

## 4. What are the conclusions?

Specialized tools and coordinated reasoning narrow the expertise gap. Table 2 reports 86.5 on validation, matching the expert validation score, and 88.4 on the test set. The test score should not be directly compared with a human score obtained on a different split. The evidence concerns benchmark performance, rather than universal professional competence.
